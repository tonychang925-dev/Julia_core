# GIC-003 — Weak-to-Strong Cognition IR

## IDENTITY / SCOPE

**Cognition unit:** `GIC-003.weak_to_strong`
**Primary question:** After a previously strong core/stock weakens or diverges, how should Julia decide whether its next-session recovery is a confirmed weak-to-strong repair candidate rather than a false reversal, ordinary fluctuation, passive market move, or displaced old leader?
**Transfer principle:** `strategy_transfer_without_conclusion_copy`

This unit is a reusable causal-reasoning scaffold. It is not a buy rule, a replay verdict, a deterministic score, a historical-performance claim, or trading authorization. Its purpose is to preserve the investigation path from a valid weakness/divergence observation to time-bound repair evidence, participation, structural recovery, hierarchy response, market gates, counterevidence, and invalidation.

Julia must preserve four layers:

1. **Observed market facts** — exact subject/date/as-of, prior strength and role, weakness quality, key level, auction/open behavior, volume/turnover, intraday recovery, follower/theme response, capital state, market regime, and provenance.
2. **Repair cognition** — whether the evidence supports a confirmed weak-to-strong candidate, false weak-to-strong, continued weakness, passive recovery, or successor displacement.
3. **Candidate setup** — a separate execution question requiring declared timing, entry/invalidation structure, liquidity, risk budget, and permission.
4. **Post-entry update** — continuation, failure, or ambiguity after the setup window, recorded without overwriting the original as-of evidence.

### Boundaries

- Weak-to-strong begins from a valid weak/divergence candidate, not from any red-to-green observation.
- Yesterday's weakness is necessary context, not sufficient evidence for today's reversal.
- A high open is not confirmation; it can be liquidity artifact, passive beta recovery, or bait.
- Strong turnover is not automatically healthy; it must support recovery rather than renewed distribution.
- A reclaimed price is not necessarily a reclaimed structure; critical level and close/auction context matter.
- One stock repair does not prove whole-theme revival.
- Theme revival does not prove the old leader remains the valid leader.
- Broad-market recovery can make an apparent repair passive rather than stock-specific.
- A failed weak-to-strong setup does not by itself prove theme death.
- Missing auction, volume, level, follower, regime, or provenance evidence yields `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, never a negative market fact.
- Historical examples transfer their reasoning structure, not their action, threshold, or claimed performance.

## FORMATION_PATH

Weak-to-strong cognition begins after a previously important stock or core becomes weak enough to create a repair question. A proposed formation path is:

```text
prior strength / leadership baseline
→ observed weakness or divergence
→ GIC-002 repair-candidate qualification
→ observation-integrity and timing gate
→ weakness-quality and key-level baseline
→ next-session auction state
→ open behavior and passive-market separation
→ volume / turnover / absorption interpretation
→ structure reclaim and intraday persistence
→ follower, theme, capital, and regime confirmation
→ competing repair hypotheses
→ setup permission and invalidation
→ post-window outcome tracking


```

### 1. Qualify the source weakness

Julia must first establish why the case is eligible for weak-to-strong reasoning:

- Which exact stock/core is under investigation?
- Was it a total leader, branch leader, core middle stock, or follower?
- What prior strength, height, relative strength, or liquidity made it important?
- Did it experience normal adjustment, active divergence, structural failure, or passive weakness?
- Is there a valid repair candidate rather than mere terminal decay or unrelated volatility?

GIC-002 owns the interpretation of leader weakness and hierarchy. GIC-003 should not be invoked merely because a declined stock becomes green.

### 2. Protect observation integrity

Every comparison requires:

- exact subject identity and share/instrument identity;
- prior-session trade date and current-session date;
- runtime `as_of` and source snapshot/version;
- matching prior/current observation windows;
- auction and continuous-session labels;
- volume/turnover units and field availability;
- explicit missing data.

No cutoff or provenance means no weak-to-strong conclusion. A later correction must update the packet through the correction path, not rewrite the original observation.

### 3. Define the weakness baseline

The meaning of repair depends on what failed:

- shallow orderly pullback versus persistent heavy selling;
- support tested versus support broken;
- one-session weakness versus repeated distribution;
- weakness after early diffusion versus weakness after attention climax;
- stock-specific weakness versus theme-wide or market-wide weakness;
- old-leader failure versus credible successor displacement.

The same next-session gain has different meaning after shallow orderly contraction and after a decisive close-based break. Julia must retain the weakness-quality baseline.

### 4. Define the reference structure

Before the current session, Julia must state the levels that make repair meaningful:

- prior critical support/resistance;
- failed level or broken structure to reclaim;
- prior close/open reference;
- expected observation window, such as auction, open, first persistence window, intraday review, and close;
- hard invalidation condition.

If the reference structure is not declared before observing recovery, the conclusion is post-hoc narrative rather than weak-to-strong cognition.

### 5. Interpret the auction

Auction evidence can establish early demand or warning, but it is provisional:

- opening above the prior weak close;
- recovery toward or above the declared level;
- auction volume/turnover relative to the subject's usable history;
- seller absence versus buyer urgency;
- gap consistency with sector, theme, and index.

A strong auction does not skip later confirmation. A weak auction can remove urgency but does not prove whole-day failure unless the declared window says so.

### 6. Separate active repair from passive recovery

Julia must compare the subject with its causal family and market:

- If index, sector, and peers recover together, part of the strength is passive beta.
- If the old leader recovers less than peers, it may be displaced even while the theme improves.
- If the subject recovers much more than family and market, stock-specific repair remains active.
- If only a different successor is strong, GIC-002 rotation—not old-leader weak-to-strong—is the relevant unit.

Passive recovery can still be tradable under a separate regime/setup decision, but it must not be labeled active weak-to-strong repair.

### 7. Interpret volume and participation

Volume must be interpreted with price location and order-flow context:

- recovery through a failed level with sustained participation supports repair;
- high-volume reversal from support can indicate absorption, if followed by persistent strength;
- high volume without price progress suggests supply remains active;
- low-volume drift upward is weaker evidence;
- extreme one-point turnover may signal crowding or manipulation risk;
- turnover capacity must be checked against liquidity and execution constraints.

Volume is evidence, not a slogan. Julia must state which behavior the turnover supports and what observation would contradict it.

### 8. Require structural persistence

A candidate becomes stronger when:

- it reclaims the declared level rather than only reducing decline;
- it holds above the level through the predeclared persistence window;
- pullbacks do not immediately lose the repaired structure;
- close location and continuation remain consistent;
- renewed selling does not reclaim control before the review boundary.

Recovery quality is more important than the largest instantaneous percentage change. A fast spike followed by failure can support the false-weak-to-strong hypothesis.

### 9. Check hierarchy and theme response

The repaired stock should remain organizationally connected to the causal theme:

- credible followers or branch stocks respond;
- theme breadth stabilizes or expands rather than only one price rises;
- event chain remains fresh or is repaired;
- capital stays in or returns to the family;
- the subject retains enough relative strength to matter in hierarchy;
- no confirmed successor has already displaced it.

Isolated old-leader strength with weak followers may be a technical rebound, capital trap, or unrelated news event. It is not automatically theme revival.

### 10. Apply regime and liquidity gates

Before promotion, Julia must record:

- index direction and broad breadth/depth;
- risk appetite and liquidity;
- whether market recovery explains most of the subject move;
- turnover migration and crowding;
- competing themes;
- event-chain contradiction;
- attention age and saturation;
- tradable liquidity versus observed turnover.

These facts gate interpretation. They must remain visible as separate evidence rather than being merged into one score.

### 11. Classify and preserve falsifiers

The output is a set of competing hypotheses with status, decisive evidence, missing evidence, and observation windows. A confirmed candidate still needs invalidation detail:

- failed level;
- time stop;
- structural stop;
- follower/theme failure;
- regime deterioration;
- source/provenance correction.

Confirmation changes candidate status, not automatic trading behavior.

## MECHANISM

The reusable mechanism is conditional repair of an organized attention/capital object:

```text
prior strength creates leadership expectation
→ weakness tests or damages that expectation
→ residual causal theme and liquidity remain live
→ next-session demand absorbs available supply
→ key structure is reclaimed and held
→ followers/theme validate hierarchy
→ attention and capital can reorganize around the core
→ continuation depends on regime, capacity, and fresh contradiction
```
``+

```

Weak-to-strong is not a moral transition from "weak" to "strong." It is a causal claim that sellers were absorbed and the prior organizing object regained conditional market authority.

### Hypothesis mechanisms

#### Confirmed weak-to-strong candidate

The prior weakness did not destroy the causal role. Next-session demand arrives at the declared level, turnover supports absorption, the failed structure is reclaimed and held, followers or theme breadth respond, and market regime does not explain the entire move.

#### False weak-to-strong

The subject shows an early gain but fails confirmation: volume does not support progress, supply returns at the level, recovery fades within the window, peers do not validate hierarchy, or the move was mostly passive market recovery. The early appearance was not durable repair.

#### Continued weakness / no repair

The weak open or early recovery fails, the declared level is not reclaimed, selling continues, followers remain weak, and capital does not return. The original weakness interpretation remains dominant.

#### Passive recovery artifact

The subject rises because the market, sector, or family rises. Its residual relative strength remains poor, and stock-specific repair is not demonstrated. This hypothesis prevents beta recovery from being mislabeled active repair.

#### Old-leader displacement

The theme may improve or rotate, but a different successor organizes followers and capital. The old leader's minor recovery does not restore its role. This state can coexist with a healthy GIC-001 lifecycle conclusion.

#### Data/provenance artifact

Auction fields, volume, prior close, level, subject identity, population, or cutoff is missing or inconsistent. The correct result is insufficiency, not a market conclusion.

| Family | Representative observations | Reasoning use |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline role | prior leadership type, relative height, liquidity, influence, prior continuation | Establish whether repair matters |
| Weakness quality | drawdown, selling persistence, support test/break, close location, repetition | Define what must be repaired |
| Structure | declared level, failed level, reclaimed level, hold time, close confirmation | Gate repair and invalidation |
| Auction | gap, auction turnover, price versus prior close/level, seller pressure | Establish early demand without final confirmation |
| Open/early session | open direction, first pullback, failure/reclaim, price persistence | Test urgency and supply |
| Participation | volume/turnover, absorption, renewal of supply, turnover capacity | Separate demand repair from drift or distribution |
| Relative strength | subject versus peers, family, sector, index | Separate active repair from passive recovery |
| Hierarchy response | follower retention, successor state, branch response, dispersion | Decide whether old authority is restored |
| Theme state | breadth, event continuity, branch formation, capital persistence | Connect repair to GIC-001 without collapsing authority |
| Market regime | index, breadth/depth, risk appetite, liquidity shock | Gate causal transfer and setup permission |
| External evidence | company/news/event contradiction, source quality, research disagreement | Challenge causal mechanism and data truth |
| Provenance | source identity, snapshot version, exact `as_of`, field lineage | Decide whether comparison is admissible |

## COMPETING_HYPOTHESES

All serious evaluations retain the canonical alternatives. A favored hypothesis must state decisive supports, active contradictions, and missing evidence.

### H-A — Confirmed weak-to-strong candidate

**Causal claim:** next-session demand absorbed prior supply and restored enough structure/hierarchy for the weak core to become a candidate setup.
**Supporting pattern**

- valid prior weakness/divergence candidate from GIC-002;
- declared critical level and observation window exist;
- auction/open behavior supports demand rather than only a mechanical gap;
- the subject reclaims and holds the declared structure;
- turnover supports absorption or renewed demand, not renewed distribution;
- subject outperforms family/index enough to remain stock-specific;
- credible followers or theme breadth respond;
- capital remains in or returns to the causal family;
- event chain is not decisively contradicted;
- market/liquidity gates do not reject the setup.

**Counterevidence**

- recovery fails before the declared window ends;
- volume rises but price cannot progress;
- market/peer recovery explains most of the move;
- followers stay weak or a successor takes authority;
- event chain is contradicted;
- source or field mismatch invalidates the comparison.

**Typical missing evidence:** auction detail, intraday persistence, usable relative-strength window, follower map, capital migration.

### H-B — False weak-to-strong

**Causal claim:** early recovery was insufficient to absorb supply or restore structure; the apparent reversal fails.
**Supporting pattern**

- gap or early gain fades;
- the failed level is approached but not reclaimed;
- reclaimed structure is quickly lost;
- high turnover accompanies renewed selling;
- follower response is absent;
- relative strength deteriorates after the open;
- the subject fails its time stop.

**Counterevidence**

- late reclamation with sustained volume and follower response;
- market regime improves and subject demonstrates excess relative strength;
- corrected data show stronger persistence than first observed.

**Typical missing evidence:** intraday path after the first peak, close confirmation, next-window follower response.

### H-C — Continued weakness / no repair

**Causal claim:** prior weakness continues; no valid repair candidate has appeared.
**Supporting pattern**

- weak auction/open;
- no meaningful attempt on the declared level;
- selling pressure persists;
- followers and theme breadth remain weak;
- capital does not return;
- the event chain remains stale or contradicted.

**Counterevidence**

- later session reclaims and holds structure with participation;
- family breadth repairs while the subject recovers;
- original weakness is explained by corrected passive-market shock.

**Typical missing evidence:** full-session close data, theme response after open, capital state.

### H-D — Passive recovery artifact

**Causal claim:** the subject recovered with market/family beta; stock-specific repair is not demonstrated.
**Supporting pattern**

- index/sector/peers recover in similar or stronger fashion;
- subject remains relatively weak;
- volume does not show distinctive demand;
- hierarchy does not return to the old leader;
- theme recovery can be organized elsewhere.

**Counterevidence**

- subject shows large excess return and volume versus family/index;
- repaired structure and follower acceptance are specific to the subject;
- passive shock reverses while subject strength persists.

**Typical missing evidence:** synchronized family/market returns, relative-strength calculation, auction attribution.

### H-E — Old-leader displacement

**Causal claim:** the family repairs, but authority has moved to a successor; the old leader's recovery does not restore its role.
**Supporting pattern**

- another same-theme successor shows stronger relative performance;
- followers accept the successor;
- capital migrates to successor/branch;
- old leader fails persistence or hierarchy checks;
- theme breadth can improve without the old leader.

**Counterevidence**

- old leader reclaims authority with superior persistence and follower response;
- successor is causally unrelated or loses acceptance;
- capital returns specifically to the old core.

**Typical missing evidence:** successor identity, branch map, follower acceptance, capital migration.

### H-F — Insufficient evidence / provenance artifact

**Causal claim:** evidence lineage or required observations are not valid enough for interpretation.
**Supporting pattern**

- missing auction/open/volume fields;
- unknown prior close or key level;
- date/subject mismatch;
- no runtime cutoff;
- population or source version changes;
- corrected data conflict with the snapshot.

**Counterevidence**

- authoritative corrected snapshot supplies the required fields and lineage.

**Consequence:** no market hypothesis is promoted. Missing is not negative evidence.

### Hypothesis-status semantics

- `SUPPORTED` — all core conditions align and decisive contradictions are absent.
- `PARTIAL` — some conditions align, but a required confirmation is still inside its observation window or evidence is missing.
- `CONTRADICTED` — a decisive condition fails, such as reclaimed structure being lost, passive recovery explaining the move, or successor displacement.
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` — required data, cutoff, or provenance is unavailable.
- `SUPERSEDED` — a later observation replaces an earlier provisional status through the correction path while preserving the audit trail.

Multiple hypotheses can coexist. For example, false weak-to-strong can be `SUPPORTED` for the old leader while GIC-001 supports theme repair through successor rotation.

## REGIME_APPLICABILITY

Regime is a gate on transfer and candidate quality, not a vocabulary decoration.

| Regime / gate | Interpretation effect |
|---|---|
| Supportive market, ample liquidity, broad/deep risk appetite | Repair requires less rescue from beta, but late-stage crowding and attention age still require caution |
| Neutral / mixed breadth | Subject-specific persistence and follower response decide |
| Adverse market or risk-off | Apparent repair needs stronger excess strength; passive recovery and trap interpretations remain live |
| Market sharply recovers | Explicitly separate beta recovery from stock-specific repair |
| Liquidity contraction | Even structurally valid repair may be non-tradable or invalid for execution |
| Theme capital migrates to successor | Old-leader repair may be too late even if price temporarily recovers |
| Event chain fresh and followers stable | Confirmation needs less rescue from broad market, but still requires level and volume |
| Event chain contradicted | Price recovery alone cannot validate the original causal mechanism |
| Attention saturation / very high prior expectation | Repair may be exit liquidity rather than renewed formation |
| Extreme turnover/crowding | Liquidity and manipulation/execution risk gates are mandatory |
| Data/provenance regime invalid | No hypothesis is promoted; return insufficient evidence |

Thresholds are intentionally not frozen here. A later implementation must expose the regime facts and gate result separately rather than merge them into a single score.

## COUNTEREVIDENCE

Counterevidence is decisive when it directly denies a core condition of the favored hypothesis.

1. **Persistence failure defeats confirmed repair.** Recovery may look strong, but loss of the declared level before the window ends demotes the candidate to false weak-to-strong.
2. **Passive beta defeats stock-specific repair.** If family/index movement explains the recovery, active repair is not demonstrated.
3. **Volume without progress defeats demand interpretation.** Persistent turnover at a failed level suggests supply remains active.
4. **Follower absence defeats hierarchy repair.** One old leader rising while credible followers stay weak does not restore theme authority.
5. **Successor acceptance defeats old-leader authority.** The theme can remain alive while the repaired stock is no longer the valid core.
6. **Event contradiction defeats narrative repair.** If the causal driver is false or stale, price recovery alone does not validate weak-to-strong.
7. **Liquidity failure defeats setup viability.** Structural repair can remain cognitively interesting while execution permission is rejected.
8. **Adverse regime defeats direct transfer.** A repair pattern calibrated in supportive conditions must not be reused unchanged in risk-off or liquidity contraction.
9. **Post-hoc level selection defeats the setup.** A level declared only after recovery is not valid confirmation evidence.
10. **Provenance mismatch defeats the comparison.** Wrong subject, unmatched dates, missing cutoff, or changed source snapshot prevents a causal claim rather than proving the opposite.
11. **Correction supersedes provisional data.** Authoritative corrected auction/volume/close evidence must update the hypothesis while preserving the original observation.

If counterevidence is decisive, Julia must demote or replace the favored hypothesis even when prior supporting evidence was strong.

## CORRECTION_PATH

1. **Record the prior status and evidence.** Do not silently replace it.
2. **Identify the correction type:** new observation, failed window, passive-market fact, hierarchy change, event contradiction, liquidity change, or data correction.
3. **Test decisive counterevidence first.** A hard contradiction overrides a favorable score.
4. **Use the predeclared level and window.** Do not create a new successful test after seeing the result.
5. **Separate missing from negative.** Unavailable evidence leaves the hypothesis insufficient; it does not prove failure.
6. **Separate price repair, hierarchy repair, and setup viability.** They can fail independently.
7. **Reopen the canonical alternatives.** Demote the favored hypothesis and promote alternatives only to the degree supported.
8. **Reapply relative-strength and regime gates.** A beta-driven move must not remain active repair.
9. **Check identity continuity.** Corporate action, renamed subject, successor, or wrong instrument must not hide a failed candidate.
10. **Update falsifiers and observation windows.** State what future fact would overturn the corrected status.
11. **Preserve provenance and audit trail.** Include source, exact date, `as_of`, subject identity, and unresolved evidence.
12. **Keep interpretation separate from action.** A corrected setup changes research, risk attention, or candidate permission, not automatic trading behavior.

## RELATIONS

### GIC-001 — Theme Lifecycle

**Boundary:** GIC-001 interprets the whole theme family and lifecycle. GIC-003 asks whether one specific weak core/stock conditionally repairs strongly enough to become a candidate setup.
**Interaction:** GIC-001 supplies event continuity, breadth, hierarchy, age, and capital context; GIC-003 supplies next-session repair evidence.
**Shared observations:** prior strength, follower response, breadth, event continuity, capital persistence, market regime.
**Different conclusions:** A confirmed subject repair can occur while the theme remains watch-only. Conversely, a theme can revive through rotation even if the old leader fails weak-to-strong confirmation.

### GIC-002 — Leader Divergence

**Boundary:** GIC-002 classifies weakness as normal adjustment, active divergence, leader failure, or leader rotation and decides whether a repair candidate exists. GIC-003 evaluates that candidate's next-session transition.
**Interaction:** GIC-002 invokes GIC-003 only after a valid repair candidate appears; GIC-003 returns confirmation, falsification, timing, and invalidation detail.
**Shared observations:** leader role, weakness quality, key level, volume, follower response, regime.
**Different conclusions:** The old leader can fail weak-to-strong while GIC-002 supports rotation, or the old leader can repair while the broader hierarchy remains unresolved.

### Market structure / regime cognition

Market breadth, depth, liquidity, and relative-strength cognition determine whether recovery is active, passive, or non-tradable. These gates can defeat a stock-only repair story.

### Event and research evidence cognition

External evidence can support or contradict the causal mechanism behind the prior leadership, but it does not replace auction/open behavior, structural recovery, or market payment.

### Risk and execution cognition

A confirmed weak-to-strong candidate is not an order. Execution requires separately declared liquidity, sizing, timing, entry structure, stop, permission, and failure handling.

## EXAMPLE_REASONING_TRACES

### Trace A — Valid repair sequence

**Facts:** A prior core leader had an orderly pullback to a declared level after GIC-002 classified active divergence. Next session it opens above that level with credible auction turnover, reclaims the failed level, holds through the first pullback, and outperforms peers and index. Followers stabilize and theme breadth stops contracting. Event chain remains fresh. Market is neutral.

**Reasoning:** H-A is supported for the declared window: structure, participation, relative strength, and hierarchy response align. H-B remains a live falsifier until the window closes. This is a candidate setup, not an order.

**Not allowed:** "Green open plus high volume always means buy" without declared level, persistence, hierarchy, and regime gates.

### Trace B — Early strength fades

**Facts:** The subject gaps higher after a deep break. It approaches the declared level, volume expands, but price cannot progress and falls back below the level before the persistence window ends. Followers remain weak.

**Reasoning:** H-B is supported: early demand did not absorb supply or restore structure. H-C may gain weight if selling continues. The failed candidate does not prove theme death.

**Not allowed:** Selecting a lower intraday level after the failure and calling the setup confirmed.

### Trace C — Passive market recovery

**Facts:** Market and theme peers recover strongly after risk-off. The old leader also rises, but its relative strength remains below family and index. Volume is not distinctive, and a successor retains follower acceptance.

**Reasoning:** H-D and H-E dominate. The old leader's move is mainly passive and its authority remains displaced. GIC-001 may still support theme repair through rotation.

**Not allowed:** Labeling every beta recovery as old-leader weak-to-strong.

### Trace D — Isolated old-leader rebound

**Facts:** The old leader reclaims its level with participation. Followers stay weak, breadth contracts, capital does not return, and the event chain is stale.

**Reasoning:** H-A is contradicted on hierarchy and causal continuity; H-B or a local technical rebound remains possible. One-stock strength does not establish theme revival.

**Not allowed:** Treating a repaired price as repaired authority.

### Trace E — Missing auction evidence

**Facts:** Prior weakness and current gain are visible, but auction/open fields, turnover, runtime cutoff, and source snapshot are missing. The key level was not predeclared.

**Reasoning:** H-F is required. There is no valid confirmation comparison, and missing fields are not evidence against repair.

**Not allowed:** Inferring confirmation from a daily close alone or filling missing auction/volume facts from narrative memory.

## ANTI_COPY_RULES

1. Do not copy a historical weak-to-strong outcome as the current answer.
2. Do not copy a prior buy/sell action from a similar reversal narrative.
3. Do not infer empirical performance from a book example, fixture, replay, or label.
4. Do not turn "weak then strong" into a deterministic two-state rule.
5. Do not collapse auction, open, level, volume, hierarchy, theme, regime, liquidity, and risk into one score.
6. Do not select or move the confirmation level after seeing the result.
7. Do not treat a green open, gap, or instantaneous gain as confirmation.
8. Do not confuse passive beta recovery with stock-specific repair.
9. Do not confuse old-leader repair with theme health or successor rotation.
10. Do not treat failed old-leader repair as whole-theme failure.
11. Do not let missing, stale, or mismatched evidence become negative evidence.
12. Do not overwrite current market facts with strategy vocabulary or historical narrative.
13. Do not infer authorization from confirmation; setup, risk, and permission are separate.
14. Do not hide the observation window, invalidation condition, or provenance.
