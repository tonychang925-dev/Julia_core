# GIC-002 — Leader Divergence Cognition IR

## IDENTITY / SCOPE

**Cognition unit:** `GIC-002.leader_divergence`
**Primary question:** When a theme leader weakens, how should Julia distinguish normal adjustment, active divergence, leader failure, and leader rotation?
**Transfer principle:** `strategy_transfer_without_conclusion_copy`

This unit is a reusable causal-reasoning scaffold. It is not a buy/sell rule, a replay verdict, a deterministic score, or permission to copy a historical trade. Its purpose is to preserve the investigation path that connects observed leader weakness to follower response, theme state, market regime, liquidity/attention, counterevidence, and falsifiers.

Julia must preserve four layers:

1. **Observed market facts** — price/return, drawdown, volume, key levels, follower behavior, breadth, capital/turnover, event continuity, market regime, and provenance for a specific subject/date/as-of.
2. **Leader interpretation** — which hypothesis best explains the leader's current weakness.
3. **Theme interpretation** — whether the broader theme remains alive, contracting, repairing, rotating, or fading; this is reasoned with GIC-001, not inferred from one leader alone.
4. **Trading action** — a separate decision requiring setup, risk, authorization, and market permission. A cognition interpretation is not an order.

### Boundaries

- "Leader weak" is an observation, not a terminal conclusion.
- Divergence is not automatically a buying opportunity.
- A broken old leader does not necessarily mean theme failure.
- A strong theme does not necessarily mean the old leader remains the correct leader.
- New strength elsewhere is not rotation until it is organizationally connected to the same theme and succeeds under regime gates.
- Broad-market weakness can make leader weakness passive rather than stock-specific.
- Unavailable or stale evidence means `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, never negative market fact.
- Historical examples transfer their reasoning structure, not their action or claimed performance.

## FORMATION_PATH

Leader-divergence cognition begins with a change in leader behavior or expectation, not with the word "divergence." A proposed formation path is:

```text
leader strength / expectation baseline
→ observable weakness or surprise break
→ observation-integrity and as-of gate
→ weakness-quality classification
→ key-level and volume interpretation
→ follower-response map
→ theme breadth and event-chain state
→ capital / liquidity / attention state
→ market-regime gate
→ successor or repair evidence
→ competing leader hypotheses
→ follow-up falsifiers
```

### 1. Establish the leader baseline

Before interpreting weakness, Julia must identify the subject, its prior role, and its expectation context:

- Is this the total leader, sector leader, middle core, or late follower?
- What height, relative strength, liquidity, or influence made it a leader?
- What continuation was priced or socially expected?
- Was prior strength isolated or supported by followers and a causal theme?
- Is the subject identity stable across the observation window?

A small-cap break and a core leader break are different evidence events. The old project's leader-death model similarly distinguishes leader role, relative height, strength, death type, and propagation rather than counting every break equally.

### 2. Observe the surprise and weakness quality

The first factual question is how the leader failed to meet expectation:

- shallow pullback versus deep drawdown;
- orderly contraction versus heavy selling;
- intraday volatility with recovery versus persistent lower closes;
- failed seal versus immediate break;
- one-session weakness versus repeated weakness;
- weakness after climax versus weakness during early diffusion.

The same percentage move can mean different things at different positions in the leader/theme lifecycle. Therefore the unit asks for temporal and structural context before classifying the event.

### 3. Protect observation integrity

Julia must bind every comparison to:

- exact stock/subject identity;
- trade date and runtime `as_of`;
- source snapshot/version;
- matching theme-constituent population;
- matching observation windows;
- explicit missing fields.

The existing Core research bindings pass one runtime cutoff into capability requests and the evaluator keeps unavailable/error evidence as missing. This unit preserves that rule: no cutoff or provenance, no comparative conclusion.

### 4. Interpret level and volume together

Price weakness is interpreted with its location and participation:

- intact support with contracting turnover can be consistent with ordinary risk release;
- testing support with elevated turnover creates an active disagreement hypothesis;
- broken support with heavy selling and close-based continuation is stronger failure evidence;
- recovery from tested support requires confirmation before becoming repair;
- a large move caused by index or sector shock must first be classified as passive pressure.

Volume is not a direction by itself. Its meaning depends on where it occurs, whether it absorbs or creates supply, and whether followers respond.

### 5. Map follower response

Leader divergence is relational. Julia must ask:

- Do credible followers hold relative strength?
- Is dispersion narrowing or expanding?
- Are second-row stocks rotating into leadership or merely producing isolated strength?
- Does weakness spread within the theme?
- Do related events/branches still receive capital?
- Is a successor connected to the same causal mechanism, or is it a different theme?

Follower resilience can defeat an old-leader failure interpretation. Follower collapse can turn an apparently ordinary adjustment into systemic failure.

### 6. Place the observation in theme state

GIC-001 supplies the theme-lifecycle context. Leader weakness has different meaning in:

- early trial — hierarchy may still be forming;
- fermentation — a first durable divergence tests whether acceptance is real;
- acceleration — late chasing has different risk than early diffusion;
- climax — weakness may be expected exhaustion;
- divergence/repair — the question becomes repair versus propagation;
- fade — leader failure may confirm terminal pressure;
- rotation — an old leader may fail while the theme remains alive.

This unit does not re-solve the whole lifecycle. It supplies the leader-side evidence GIC-001 needs.

### 7. Apply market and liquidity/attention gates

The same leader fact can be caused by idiosyncratic leadership conflict or by market-wide pressure. Julia must inspect:

- broad-market direction, breadth, and depth;
- risk-appetite state;
- available liquidity and turnover migration;
- competing themes or sink assets;
- crowding, position saturation, and attention age;
- event-chain freshness or contradiction.

These are gates on interpretation, not additive score components to hide.

### 8. Seek successor and repair evidence

Before choosing rotation or repair, Julia must distinguish:

- **Candidate successor** — new strength exists.
- **Confirmed successor** — same theme mechanism, follower acceptance, liquidity, and relative strength persist for the declared window.
- **Repair candidate** — old leader stabilizes at a key level.
- **Confirmed repair** — recovery survives volume, follower, and regime checks.

GIC-003 owns the detailed weak-to-strong transition. GIC-002 only identifies when that unit becomes relevant.

## MECHANISM

The reusable mechanism is expectation conflict inside a hierarchical, liquidity-constrained market:

```text
leader previously organized attention and capital
→ new information or profit pressure changes expected continuation
→ participants reprice the leader
→ follower/theme response reveals whether the conflict is local or structural
→ liquidity and regime determine repair/rotation capacity
→ old-leader state and theme state can diverge
```

### Hypothesis mechanisms

#### Normal adjustment

Prior strength created profit and attention. Some holders sell, turnover contracts or remains orderly, the leader remains above critical structure, and followers/theme breadth stay intact. The causal driver of the theme is unchanged. Weakness is a price/risk-release event, not evidence that leadership has failed.

#### Active divergence

Participants disagree about continuation near a decision level. Price tests support, turnover rises, follower response becomes mixed, and theme breadth stops expanding or begins contracting. The disagreement is informative but unresolved. The correct cognition is conditional monitoring, not immediate reversal or purchase.

#### Leader failure

The leader loses the structural and participatory conditions that made it a leader: key structure breaks, selling persists, followers weaken, theme breadth contracts, and capital leaves rather than rotates. The old leader may no longer be a valid organizing core even if some related stocks remain alive.

#### Leader rotation

The old leader weakens while capital and attention move to a credible successor in the same causal theme. Theme breadth, event continuity, or successor relative strength remains sufficient. This is not theme health by itself; it is a changing hierarchy. The old leader and theme can have different conclusions.

#### Passive divergence overlay

Passive divergence is not a fifth terminal state. It is a market-causal overlay: broad-market or liquidity shock makes the leader weak independently of its own leadership quality. Julia must recognize this before treating weakness as stock-specific failure or as a contrarian opportunity.

### Evidence families

| Family | Representative observations | Reasoning use |
|---|---|---|
| Leader strength | return, drawdown, board/height continuation, close location, surprise versus expected continuation | Characterize weakness quality |
| Participation | volume/turnover trend, seal/failure behavior, intraday recovery, absorption | Separate orderly release from selling pressure |
| Structure | key level state, support/test/break, close confirmation, recovery quality | Gate damage and repair |
| Follower response | peer relative strength, dispersion, follower retention, propagation, successor acceptance | Decide local versus systemic interpretation |
| Theme state | breadth trend, branch formation, event continuity, mainline lifecycle, new leader count | Connect leader to theme without collapsing authority |
| Capital/attention | capital persistence, outflow, turnover migration, crowding, attention age, competing themes | Determine capacity to repair or rotate |
| Market regime | index direction, broad breadth/depth, risk appetite, liquidity shock | Apply passive-divergence and permission gates |
| External evidence | company/news/event contradiction, source quality, research disagreement | Challenge causal mechanism and data truth |
| Provenance | source identity, snapshot version, exact `as_of`, population identity | Decide whether comparison is admissible |

## COMPETING_HYPOTHESES

All serious evaluations retain the four canonical alternatives. A favored hypothesis must state its decisive supports, active contradictions, and missing evidence.

### H-A — Normal adjustment

**Causal claim:** profit/risk release has not damaged leadership structure or theme organization.
**Supporting pattern**

- drawdown remains shallow and orderly for the declared window;
- critical level remains intact;
- volume contracts or decline participation is not persistent heavy selling;
- credible followers retain relative strength;
- theme breadth and event chain remain stable or expanding;
- market regime is not forcing passive weakness.

**Counterevidence**

- close-based support break;
- repeated distribution after recovery attempts;
- follower dispersion expands into weakness;
- breadth contracts;
- capital leaves the theme;
- external evidence contradicts the causal mechanism.

**Typical missing evidence:** capital persistence, next-window confirmation, high-quality follower map.

### H-B — Active divergence

**Causal claim:** participants actively disagree about continuation near a decision point; the outcome is unresolved.
**Supporting pattern**

- leader tests a meaningful level;
- turnover is elevated on the decline/test;
- follower response is mixed rather than uniformly strong or weak;
- theme breadth stops expanding or contracts moderately;
- liquidity remains sufficient for either repair or rotation;
- event chain is neither clearly exhausted nor clearly contradicted.

**Counterevidence**

- broad-market shock explains most leader/follower weakness;
- level remains intact and turnover contracts, favoring normal adjustment;
- support breaks with propagation, favoring failure;
- confirmed same-theme successor accepts capital, favoring rotation;
- old leader recovers with follower confirmation, resolving divergence.

**Typical missing evidence:** next-session repair/continuation, successor confirmation, capital allocation detail.

### H-C — Leader failure

**Causal claim:** the old leader has lost the structural, participatory, or causal conditions of leadership.
**Supporting pattern**

- critical structure breaks and weakness persists beyond one observation;
- volume/selling pressure is heavy rather than orderly;
- followers weaken and propagation is visible;
- theme breadth contracts;
- capital exits rather than rotates;
- event chain is stale, contradicted, or no longer supports the old leader's premium.

**Counterevidence**

- broad market alone explains the move;
- followers remain strong and breadth stable;
- old leader repairs key structure during the declared window;
- credible successor emerges in same theme, changing the conclusion to old-leader failure plus rotation rather than whole-theme failure;
- provenance or population mismatch invalidates the comparison.

**Typical missing evidence:** propagation quality, capital outflow, event-chain status, close confirmation.

### H-D — Leader rotation

**Causal claim:** leadership is changing within the same causal theme; old-leader weakness and theme viability can coexist.
**Supporting pattern**

- old leader weakens;
- one or more successor candidates show credible relative strength;
- successor belongs to the same event/mechanism/branch rather than an unrelated theme;
- followers or breadth stabilize;
- capital persists in the theme and may migrate to the successor;
- event chain remains fresh enough to support continuation.

**Counterevidence**

- successor strength is isolated and has no follower acceptance;
- successor belongs to a different causal mechanism;
- theme breadth and event chain decay while old leader weakens;
- capital exits the whole theme;
- old leader recovers and reclaims leadership before successor confirmation.

**Typical missing evidence:** successor identity, successor persistence window, branch/follower map, capital migration.

### Hypothesis-status semantics

- `SUPPORTED` — core conditions align and decisive contradictions are absent.
- `PARTIAL` — some core conditions align, but required evidence is missing or one non-decisive conflict exists.
- `CONTRADICTED` — a decisive condition fails or the evidence pattern conflicts.
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` — required observations are unavailable, stale, or mismatched.

These statuses organize research; they do not map directly to buy, sell, or hold.

## REGIME_APPLICABILITY

Regime is a gate on causal transfer, not a vocabulary decoration.

| Regime / gate | Interpretation effect |
|---|---|
| Supportive market, broad/deep participation, ample liquidity | Same-leader shallow weakness is more plausibly adjustment, but crowded late-stage height still requires divergence/resolution checks |
| Neutral / mixed breadth | Active divergence and rotation remain live; successor and capital evidence decide |
| Adverse market or risk-off | Passive divergence is strongly live; do not over-attribute weakness to leader failure |
| Liquidity contraction / turnover migration away from theme | Repair capacity falls; failure and fade interpretations gain weight |
| Attention saturation / very high prior expectation | Late weakness may be expected exhaustion; normal-adjustment confidence falls |
| Event chain fresh and followers stable | Rotation or repair remain credible even if old leader is damaged |
| Event chain contradicted or exhausted | Leader weakness gains causal support; adjustment/rotation require stronger counterevidence |
| Competing theme absorbs liquidity | Rotation may be cross-theme rather than within-theme; verify causal identity before calling it GIC-002 rotation |
| Data/provenance regime invalid | No interpretation is promoted; return insufficient evidence |

Regime thresholds are intentionally not frozen here. A later implementation must expose the regime facts and gate outcome separately rather than merge them into a single score.

## COUNTEREVIDENCE

Counterevidence is decisive when it directly denies a core condition of the favored hypothesis.

1. **Follower resilience defeats old-leader failure.** The leader breaks, but credible followers retain strength, breadth stabilizes, and capital remains. Conclusion moves toward old-leader damage plus possible rotation, not whole-theme failure.
2. **Successor acceptance defeats theme-death inference.** Same-theme successor strength plus follower acceptance supports rotation even if the original leader fails.
3. **Broad-market shock defeats stock-specific interpretation.** If index/sector pressure explains most weakness, passive divergence remains active and leader-failure confidence must fall.
4. **Orderly contraction defeats active-divergence urgency.** Intact structure, contracting volume, and stable followers favor normal adjustment over urgent conflict.
5. **Propagation defeats normal adjustment.** Support break, persistent selling, follower weakness, and breadth contraction can overturn a shallow-drawdown story.
6. **Capital exit defeats rotation.** New price strength without capital/follower acceptance is not confirmed leadership rotation.
7. **Event contradiction defeats narrative repair.** If the causal driver is refuted or stale, recovery of price alone does not validate the original leadership mechanism.
8. **Provenance mismatch defeats the comparison.** Different dates, constituent populations, source snapshots, or missing cutoffs prevent a causal claim rather than proving the opposite.
9. **Repair confirmation defeats terminal failure.** Reclaimed structure plus sustained recovery and follower response can demote failure, subject to GIC-003 confirmation gates.

If counterevidence is decisive, Julia must demote or replace the favored interpretation even when prior supporting evidence was strong.

## FALSIFIERS

Each falsifier needs a predeclared observation window and exact-date evidence. The following are concrete cognition falsifiers, not performance claims.

| ID | Favored interpretation | Falsifying observation | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| LD-F01 | Normal adjustment | Leader closes below the declared critical level and weakness persists in the next observation window | Promote active divergence or leader failure |
| LD-F02 | Normal adjustment | Follower weakness spreads and theme breadth contracts through the window | Reinterpret as propagation/failure, not local release |
| LD-F03 | Active divergence | Leader reclaims the tested level with confirmed volume and follower response | Resolve toward repair/normal continuation using GIC-003 |
| LD-F04 | Active divergence | Support breaks while heavy selling and peer weakness continue | Promote leader-failure hypothesis |
| LD-F05 | Leader failure | Old leader repairs structure or a same-theme successor organizes breadth/capital | Separate old-leader failure from theme death; consider rotation |
| LD-F06 | Leader rotation | No successor retains follower acceptance and liquidity over the declared window | Reinterpret as old-leader failure/theme fade rather than rotation |
| LD-F07 | Leader rotation | Apparent successor belongs to a different causal mechanism | Reclassify as cross-theme opportunity, not within-theme rotation |
| LD-F08 | Passive divergence | Peers, breadth, and capital weaken much more than market beta explains | Restore stock/theme-specific failure hypothesis |
| LD-F09 | Any interpretation | Corrected source, snapshot, or population invalidates a core comparison | Return `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`; do not infer the opposite |

## CORRECTION_PATH

1. **Record the prior interpretation**, its favored evidence, contradictions, missing fields, and decision time.
2. **Classify the new observation:** leader price/participation, structure, follower response, theme breadth, capital, regime, event truth, or provenance.
3. **Check admissibility first.** Date, `as_of`, subject identity, source version, and population must match before comparison.
4. **Test decisive counterevidence first.** A hard contradiction overrides a favorable aggregate score.
5. **Separate missing from negative.** Unavailable evidence cannot prove failure or repair.
6. **Re-open all competing hypotheses** to the degree supported, not only the nearest neighboring state.
7. **Separate old leader and theme.** Update two conclusions when needed: old leader failed; theme may rotate or remain alive.
8. **Separate candidate from confirmation.** A new strong stock or one recovery bar is a candidate, not a validated successor/repair.
9. **Reapply regime and liquidity gates.** A repair/rotation story calibrated in a supportive regime must not survive unchanged in risk-off or liquidity contraction.
10. **Update falsifiers and observation windows.** State what future fact would overturn the corrected interpretation.
11. **Preserve provenance and audit trail.** Include source, exact date, subject identity, and unresolved evidence.
12. **Keep interpretation separate from action.** A corrected leader state changes research and risk attention, not automatic trading behavior.

## RELATIONS

### GIC-001 — Theme Lifecycle

**Boundary:** GIC-001 interprets the whole theme family and lifecycle. GIC-002 interprets the meaning of a specific leader's weakness and hierarchy change.
**Interaction:** GIC-002 supplies leader-side evidence to GIC-001's divergence, repair, rotation, and fade questions. GIC-001 supplies breadth, event continuity, branch formation, and lifecycle context.
**Shared observations:** leader strength, follower response, successor formation, theme breadth, capital persistence, market regime, and liquidity.
**Different conclusions:** "old leader failed" and "theme remains alive" can both be true when rotation is credible. Conversely, "leader remains intact" does not alone prove theme health.

### GIC-003 — Weak-to-Strong

**Boundary:** GIC-002 detects and classifies divergence, including whether repair or succession is a live alternative. GIC-003 reasons about whether a specific weak core/stock conditionally repairs strongly enough to become a candidate setup.
**Interaction:** GIC-002 invokes GIC-003 only after a repair candidate appears; GIC-003 supplies auction/open confirmation, weakness quality, volume/support, timing, and invalidation detail.
**Shared observations:** prior strength, key level, weakness quality, repair attempt, volume confirmation, follower/theme state, and regime.
**Different conclusions:** A theme can rotate away from an old leader even if that leader fails weak-to-strong confirmation. Conversely, one confirmed weak-to-strong repair does not by itself prove whole-theme re-acceleration.

### Market structure / regime cognition

Market breadth, depth, liquidity, and regime determine whether leader weakness is idiosyncratic, passive, or systemic. They can defeat a stock-only interpretation.

### Event and research evidence cognition

External evidence can support or contradict the causal mechanism behind leadership, but it does not replace market payment, follower response, or structural evidence.

## EXAMPLE_REASONING_TRACES

These traces transfer reasoning structure only. They do not prescribe answers or actions.

### Trace A — Shallow weakness in intact structure

**Facts:** A high-position leader pulls back modestly. Its declared key level remains intact. Turnover contracts. Credible followers hold relative strength. Theme breadth is stable. Market regime is supportive.

**Reasoning:** The observation is consistent with profit release rather than structural break. H-A is currently favored, but H-B remains live if the level is tested with elevated participation. Julia should request follower dispersion, capital persistence, and next-window level behavior.

**Not allowed:** "Leader is weak, so fade the theme" or "leader will necessarily repair tomorrow."

### Trace B — Level test with mixed followers

**Facts:** The leader tests a critical level on elevated volume. Some followers weaken while others retain strength. Theme positive breadth contracts. Capital has not clearly left. Market regime is mixed.

**Reasoning:** H-B is favored because active disagreement, decision location, and mixed hierarchy align. H-D remains possible if a successor organizes followers; H-C is not yet favored because propagation and capital exit are incomplete. Julia should distinguish old-leader state from theme state and inspect next-window repair/rotation.

**Not allowed:** Treating the test as an automatic low-buy or treating one weak close as confirmed leader failure.

### Trace C — Broad-market pressure

**Facts:** The leader falls while the index and most peers also fall. Liquidity contracts across themes. The leader remains above its declared critical level. Event chain is intact.

**Reasoning:** Passive divergence is the leading overlay. H-C loses stock-specific support because market beta can explain the weakness. H-A/H-B remain conditional. Julia should compare residual leader/follower weakness after regime evidence and avoid a contrarian buy solely because the theme narrative remains attractive.

**Not allowed:** "Bad-market divergence is always buyable" without separate repair and regime confirmation.

### Trace D — Old leader breaks while successor forms

**Facts:** The old leader breaks its key level and continues weak. A same-branch successor shows repeated relative strength and follower acceptance. Theme breadth stabilizes and capital remains in the family. Market regime is not risk-off.

**Reasoning:** H-C explains the old leader, while H-D explains the theme hierarchy. These conclusions coexist. GIC-001 should not infer whole-theme death from the old leader alone; GIC-003 is relevant only if the old leader or another core shows a valid repair candidate.

**Not allowed:** Requiring the old leader to recover before the theme can remain alive, or treating every new strong stock as a confirmed successor without follower and causal-identity checks.

### Trace E — New strength without acceptance

**Facts:** The old leader weakens. One unrelated or thinly connected stock rises. Followers do not respond. Theme breadth and capital persistence decline. Event chain is stale.

**Reasoning:** H-D is contradicted or insufficient: candidate succession lacks same-theme causal identity and acceptance. H-C plus theme fade gains weight. Julia should not label the isolated strength as rotation.

**Not allowed:** "Some stock is strong, therefore leader rotation."
