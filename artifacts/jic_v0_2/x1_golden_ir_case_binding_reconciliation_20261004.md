### Why GIC-002 is unsupported

The cases do not expose an exact leader identity and role, prior leader baseline, weakness quality, drawdown/support, volume or turnover, follower response, regime/liquidity gate, successor evidence, or a declared observation window. A subject label or event mapping is not leader-divergence evidence.

### Why GIC-003 is unsupported

The cases do not expose a prior strong/weak baseline, declared repair level, auction/open behavior, volume participation, intraday recovery, structural persistence, follower/theme response, or next-window confirmation. A same-day event or post-market description cannot be promoted to weak-to-strong repair evidence.

### Why only limited GIC-001 bindings are retained

The 2026-07-19 and 2026-09-30 cases contain source-pinned event stimulus observations and explicit candidate mechanisms. They can support a narrowly scoped question about whether an event creates a theme candidate. They cannot support a full lifecycle state, acceptance, diffusion, divergence, repair, or fade conclusion because market acceptance and regime observations are absent.

The 2026-10-02 case is rejected even for GIC-001 because its visible news set is heterogeneous and no coherent theme lifecycle object is source-pinned.
