### Proven Tags (all tested successfully)

### Tags That Don't Work

### Best Combinations
- Moaning: [呻吟] (beats all English equivalents)
- Breathing/gasping: [突然大声尖叫] + 啊... pattern
- Kissing: [呻吟] muah... muah... (dots for spacing)
- Laughing: [笑] or [大笑] + 哈哈哈
- Intimate whisper: [whispers]
- Sad: [sad]

### Critical Punctuation Rules
- `.` = short pause (quick breath)
- `...` = long pause (drawn out exhale)
- `~` = elongated vowel (softens)
- No punctuation = TTS reads as continuous speech
- No space between tag and first character = more natural

### TTS Limitations
- Cannot sing melody (no pitch variation)
- 啵啵/啾啾啾/吧唧 all read as words, not kissing sounds
- muah... muah... is the best approximation of kissing
